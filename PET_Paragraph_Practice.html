    Example

    How does Grahame show that Mole prefers being outdoors?

    Point
    Grahame presents the outdoors as a place of relief for Mole after the discomfort of cleaning.
    Evidence
    Grahame describes the moment Mole steps outside in the line “soft breezes caressed his heated brow”.
    Technique
    Grahame personifies the breeze by giving it the human action of caressing.
